The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on #4471 writes the day first, so a date with day 12 or lower comes out swapped. Any channel, every tenant.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only the form channel, only booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, it does not swap. “Tuesday showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” This looks like time zones.
